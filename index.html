Mason Lynaugh

Executive Director, Stand With Crypto

He builds the political infrastructure that makes crypto voters a defining constituency, not a talking point.

Now

Mason is executive director of Stand With Crypto, America’s largest crypto advocacy organization. He was the group’s first hire, spent two years as community director, and took the ED seat in March 2026.

He is the primary messenger to lawmakers: fly-ins, chapter presidents in the room, and a ground game built to hold politicians accountable at the ballot box.

The machine

As community director he grew SWC’s grassroots to more than 2.7 million advocates, put state chapters in all 50 states, and launched a college chapter program with 37 chapters across 21 states. In 2025 those chapters ran more than 600 local events, including Unlock the Block parties that put crypto users in the same room as lawmakers and small businesses.

Austin

Before SWC, Mason co-founded ATX DAO to make Austin a crypto hub: community, local artists, and elected officials who actually understood the tech. Forbes named him to its first 30 Under 30 Local Austin list in 2023.

He came up in Austin and graduated from the University of Texas at Austin with a BBA in Management Information Systems.
